Reference

Key signatures

  • Sharp keys: the last sharp is the leading tone. The major key is a half step above it.
  • Flat keys: the second-to-last flat is the major key. One flat is F major.
  • Minor keys: the relative minor is a minor third below the major key, on scale degree 6.

Figured bass

  • Triads: root position 53 (no figure), first inversion 6, second inversion 64.
  • Seventh chords: root position 7, first inversion 65, second inversion 43, third inversion 42.

Chord quality

  • Roman numerals: upper case for major (I, IV, V), lower case for minor (ii, iii, vi), ° for diminished, ø for half-diminished, M for a major seventh (IM7).
  • Chord symbols: C, Cm, Cdim, C7, Cmaj7, Cm7, Cm7♭5, Cdim7. A slash shows the bass note in an inversion (C/E).
  • Minor keys: V, V7, vii°, and vii°7 use the raised leading tone (harmonic minor). The other chords use natural minor.

Structure

  • Close: less than an octave between the soprano and the tenor.
  • Open: an octave or more between the soprano and the tenor.
